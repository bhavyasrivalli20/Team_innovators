Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Why do I feel stressed frequently?

Stress can happen because of work pressure, personal challenges, or emotional changes. Relaxation practices, proper sleep, and sharing feelings can help manage stress.

Q2. How can meditation improve mental health?

Meditation trains your mind to stay focused and calm. It improves emotional control and helps handle difficult situations.

Q3. Is feeling emotionally tired normal?

Yes. Emotional tiredness can happen after continuous stress. Self-care and support from loved ones can help.

Q4. When should I seek professional help?

If stress, sadness, or anxiety affects daily life for a long time, professional support can help.
