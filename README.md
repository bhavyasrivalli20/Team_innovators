# Team_innovators
AI-powered women's wellness platform for personalized health, nutrition, fitness, mental well-being, and multilingual awareness.
SAHELI – Women’s Wellness Ecosystem
The Idea
Saheli is a comprehensive women-centric wellness platform that unifies menstrual wellness, medication management, nutrition, mental well-being, fitness, and creative engagement into a single ecosystem. The project addresses the problem of fragmented healthcare applications by providing personalized wellness support through logic-based recommendations and Python-powered medication tracking.

Documentation

Problem Statement:
Women often use multiple applications for period tracking, medication reminders, nutrition planning, fitness, and wellness support. This fragmented approach reduces convenience and personalization.

Existing System:
Current platforms focus only on individual domains such as period tracking, medicine reminders, fitness, or nutrition. No unified platform provides holistic women's wellness support.

Proposed Solution:
Saheli integrates health, wellness, nutrition, medication management, fitness, and creativity into one ecosystem.

How It Works:
Users interact with different wellness modules. Inputs are processed through rule-based logic systems that generate personalized recommendations, wellness suggestions, nutrition guidance, and medication insights.

Unique Innovations:
• Hostel-Friendly Nutrition Guidance based on available ingredients.
• Python-Powered Medication Tracker with adherence monitoring.
• Logic-Based Recommendation Engine.
• Integrated Women's Wellness Ecosystem.

Impact:
Improves medication adherence, menstrual health awareness, nutrition habits, preventive healthcare, mental well-being, and overall lifestyle management.

Conclusion:
Saheli serves as a trusted digital wellness companion that empowers women through accessible, practical, and personalized wellness support.
