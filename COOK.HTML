    Power-Packed Microwave Oatmeal

    Complex Carbs & Fiber ❌ Zero Oil

    A fast, hearty formula designed to provide steady energy release without causing heavy blood sugar crashes during long mornings.

    Health Benefits: High in beta-glucan fiber, which improves gut motility, stabilizes glucose levels, and provides steady energy for hours.

    The Process:

    1. Mix 1/2 cup of oats with 1 cup of water or low-fat milk in a large mug.
    2. Microwave on high for 90 to 120 seconds until thick.
    3. Stir in 1 tablespoon of raw peanut butter and a sliced banana for natural sweetness and magnesium.

    Steamed Sprouted Moong Salad

    High Plant Protein ❌ Zero Oil

    A fresh, crisp, no-cook option packed with vitamin C to naturally boost iron absorption.

    Health Benefits: Raw sprouts are highly bioavailable, which helps combat fatigue and improves red blood cell count.

    The Process:

    1. Rinse 1 cup of pre-sprouted moong beans with clean drinking water.
    2. Add finely chopped cucumber, raw onions, and tomatoes into a bowl.
    3. Toss together with lemon juice, a pinch of black salt, and roasted cumin powder. Skip all oil dressings completely.

    Kettle-Boiled Tangy Black Chana

    Iron & Lean Protein ❌ Zero Oil

    An excellent, savory evening snack designed specifically for hostellers using nothing but a standard electric kettle.

    Health Benefits: Loaded with iron and dietary fiber, which helps curb unhealthy snack cravings and supports long-term reproductive health.

    The Process:

    1. Boil pre-soaked black chana inside your electric kettle for 15-20 minutes until tender.
    2. Drain the water completely and transfer the hot chana to a clean bowl.
    3. Season with chaat masala, freshly squeezed lime, and raw chopped green chillies.

    No-Fry Steamed Vegetable Poha

    Light & Easily Digestible ❌ Zero Oil

    A classic, light breakfast modified to preserve nutrients by steaming vegetables instead of frying them in oil.

    Health Benefits: Easily digestible carbohydrates that give you an instant energy boost without making you feel sluggish or heavy.

    The Process:

    1. Dry-toast 3 tablespoons of oats in a pan or microwave until crunchy (no oil needed).
    2. Whisk 1 cup of fresh, plain low-fat curd until smooth.
    3. Layer the toasted oats over the curd and finish with a handful of fresh pomegranate seeds or chopped almonds.
